Design system · Chinmay Sharma · 2026

Modern.

Loud type, warm paper, colour as blocks, and soft-cornered icons. The structure follows the existing Paper & Ink system; the visual language is rebuilt from thebranchmedia.org.

Values marked ◆ were read from that site's theme stylesheet. Everything else — scales, rules, components, icons, motion — is designed here.

01

Principles

Six rules that settle arguments. When two options conflict, prefer the one that keeps text readable, works without motion or hover, and uses fewer colours.

01

Say it loud

One statement per screen, set in heavy uppercase. A headline that needs a second clause becomes a second headline.

02

Paper first, colour as event

Paper is the canvas. Colour arrives as full blocks — a band, a hover fill, a CTA — never as sprinkles.

03

Hard edges, soft ends

2px ink rules and bordered blocks. Buttons, tags and icon terminals are round. Nothing in this system has a sharp corner you can cut yourself on.

04

Evidence in plain language

Declarative first person. Every claim traces to portfolio content.

05

Motion explains, never decorates

Each animation answers one question: where did that come from, what can I press, or what just changed.

06

Contrast is a rule, not a taste

Every pair is measured before it ships. Section 02 is binding.

Non-text contrast

A yellow fill is 1.48:1 against paper — invisible as a boundary. It is allowed only because its edge is a 2px ink border at 11.23:1. Never let an accent fill be the only thing defining an edge.

Do
  • Put deep text on every accent fill.
  • One accent per section.
  • Pair colour with a glyph for state.
Don't
  • Orange, green, yellow or pink as text on paper.
  • Paper text on orange (2.64:1).
  • Colour as the only signal of an error.

Type in motion — letter animation

Type animates at one of three levels: line, word or letter. The level is chosen by how much there is to read, never by how it looks. Switch the split below and watch the same headline at each level.

Split level

Reduced motion on — text renders whole, never split80ms stagger · mask rise · --dur-slow · --ease-out

Evidence overassumption.
Line — the default

Each line rises out of a clipping mask. Reads as one gesture. Use for every headline over three words, and for any text a person must actually read.

Word — leads only

Acceptable for a lead paragraph of one or two sentences. Never for body copy: the eye cannot scan text that is still assembling.

Letter — three words maximum

Display only, and only where the word is the point — a hero name, a single CTA. At 25ms a ten-letter word already costs 250ms before the last letter lands.

Hover — per-letter styles, display links only

All six stagger by 20ms and fire on focus as well as hover. None of them may carry information, and none belongs on body text, buttons or anything under 18px.

Filled or outline

Do
  • Fill the subject, outline the context. Fill carries mass; outline carries structure.
  • Use outline-only at small sizes, in dense layouts, and inside prose.
  • Use filled for empty states, feature blocks and anywhere the illustration is the focal point.
Don't
  • Fill everything — it becomes a blob with no hierarchy.
  • Mix filled and outline pieces in the same view.
  • Use fill to indicate state; that is the component's job.

The page-turn caveat

It is included so the argument only happens once. A 3D turn is skeuomorphic: it claims the content is paper, and at 700ms it is the slowest transition here. It is allowed only where the metaphor is true — a slide deck, a multi-page PDF reader. It is banned on routes. If you want weight on a route change, use Cover.

Speed rules

Do
  • Speed follows distance and area, never importance. An important page does not deserve a slower transition.
  • Exits run about 30% faster than entrances, with an ease-in curve, so the old view gets out of the way.
  • Shorten on repeat visits (× .75) and on phones (× .85).
  • A new navigation interrupts the one in flight, from where it currently is.
  • If the destination is not ready in 100ms, cross-fade at 200ms and let content arrive.
Don't
  • Exceed 700ms on a route, or 1s anywhere without a progress indicator.
  • Use the distance formula below about 25% — the clamp makes every small move 350ms. Use the 200ms cross-fade.
  • Slow a transition to make it feel premium; people read that as lag.
  • Let the transition wait on the network. Time is the cost, not the garnish.

14

Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor, and this system is stricter in two places: 44px targets and measured non-text contrast.

Binding rules
  • Contrast table in 02 — text 4.5:1, large 3:1, non-text 3:1.
  • Focus: 3px deep outline, 2px offset; yellow on dark. Never removed.
  • Targets ≥ 44px (WCAG 2.5.8 asks 24px).
  • One h1, ordered headings, landmarks, skip link first.
  • No colour-only state; every state pairs with a glyph or text.
  • Reduced motion removes transforms and pointer effects.
Verification pass
  • axe clean on every template.
  • Keyboard-only walk of each flow, including the overlay menu.
  • VoiceOver pass on hero, work index and contact form.
  • Reduced-motion on and off.
  • 200% zoom with no horizontal scroll at 320px.

Focus is visible on every surface

Voice

First person, declarative, under 20 words a sentence. Headlines take a position; buttons use verbs.

Do

Evidence over assumption.

  • “17 participants across 3 geographies.”
  • “Say hello” · “Download résumé”
  • Errors: what happened, then what to do.
Don't

“Passionate designer leveraging synergies to craft delightful experiences.”

  • “Various stakeholders” — name them.
  • “Learn more” — say what is being learned.
  • Numbers without a unit or a source.

18–21

Governance & delivery

How the system changes, what done means, and what is still outstanding.

Definition of done
  • Matches its state table.
  • Uses tokens only.
  • Passes the section 14 checks.
  • Tested with reduced motion.
  • Verified at 320, 768 and 1440.
  • Documented in the markdown file.
Change rules
  • A new colour needs a measured contrast row.
  • A new motion needs a purpose, tokens and a reduced-motion behaviour.
  • A new icon needs the construction sheet in section 06.
  • Deviations are documented, never coded silently.

Outstanding

  • Licensed reference fonts (Manuka, Financier Display, Basis Grotesque Pro) are not bundled — Inter and Cormorant stand in.
  • Dark mode is out of scope for v1.0.
